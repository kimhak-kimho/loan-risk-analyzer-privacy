Privacy Policy

Loan Risk Analyzer (វិភាគហានិភ័យកម្ចី)

Last updated: 26 September 2026

What this app is

This app is an educational loan-affordability and risk estimate for people in Cambodia. It is not a bank or microfinance institution and does not approve or reject loans.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal identity data. The app does not ask for your name, phone number, ID, passport, bank account, or card number.

Numbers you type (income, expenses, loan amount, and similar) stay on your phone. History is stored only on the device. There is no login and no server.

Internet

The app can use the internet only when you tap Facebook or Telegram in Settings, to open those links. We do not send your loan figures to those sites.

Ads, analytics, and children

No ads, no analytics SDKs, and no sale of data. The app is not directed at children under 13.
